# Diffusion Studio UI Design System

## Overview
Dark cinematic professional video editor aesthetic. Sleek minimal toolbars, crisp borders, subtle glassmorphism, and responsive micro-interactions.

## Colors
- Primary: Vivid Blue `#3b82f6`
- Dark Canvas / Background: `#121214`
- Surface & Cards: `#1a1a1e`
- Borders: Crisp zinc `#27272a`
- Text: High contrast `#f4f4f5` and muted zinc `#a1a1aa`
